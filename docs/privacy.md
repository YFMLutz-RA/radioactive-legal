# Privacy Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use a mobile application branded by a participating Station (the “Service” or “app”).

The Service is built and operated by RadioActive Expectations LLC (“RadioActive,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) on behalf of independently operated Stations.

By using the Service, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect limited information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the Service. The types of information we may collect include:

- Account information, such as an email address or username, if you choose to create an account
- Usage information, including audio playback activity and interactions with features while audio is playing
- Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and basic diagnostic data

RadioActive does not sell personal information.

## 2. Deals, Coupons, Contests, and Promotions

If the Service includes optional deals, coupons, contests, or promotions, limited information may be collected when a user voluntarily participates or redeems an offer. This information is used to validate participation or redemption and to support the operation of station-specific features.

RadioActive does not sell this information or use it for behavioral advertising. Participating Stations may use information related to promotions in accordance with their own policies and applicable law.

Participation in these features is optional and not required to use the Service.

## 3. Analytics and Advertising

The Service uses basic analytics to understand how users interact with the Service, including aggregate listening activity and engagement with features while audio is playing. This information is used to operate features, support optional non-monetary rewards or participation features, and improve functionality. Analytics are limited to first-party data collected within the Service.

The Service may include third-party advertising, such as ads provided by Google AdMob or similar services. These third-party advertising providers may collect information directly in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable platform requirements. RadioActive does not control how third-party advertisers collect or use data.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared in the following circumstances:

- With service providers that assist in operating or supporting the Service
- With the Station whose branding appears in the app
- With third-party advertising partners, as applicable
- When required to comply with law, regulation, or legal process

Participating Stations are independent operators. RadioActive does not control and is not responsible for how Stations use or manage information made available to them, which may be subject to the Station’s own privacy policies and applicable law.
